Our Beautiful Journey ✨

Our Destiny 🌸

I never imagined that going to that dandiya night would lead me to the person who would one day rule my heart and become the most beautiful part of my life. It still feels unreal how one random night slowly turned into my favorite story.

First Time I Saw U 🌙

I still remember that day I was at the wedding and I was completely bored. Suddenly Rini added me to a random GC. I opened the group just to pass time and started checking the members… and then I saw ur profile. Funny how one random notification slowly became the beginning of my favorite story.

The First Talk ✨

After that, our daily snapchat talks slowly became a habit and ur humor made me realize how someone could be so effortlessly interesting. Every conversation with u felt different and without even noticing I started looking forward to talking to u every single day.

Ups & Downs 🌌

One day, out of nowhere u said that maybe we shouldn't talk anymore… and then u just disappeared. But somehow, no matter how many times distance came between us, we always found our way back. It felt like some invisible thread connecting us, pulling us closer every time we drifted apart.

Birthday ✨

On 6th January, u decided that maybe we should stop talking. After that, I felt like I was just existing without a soul. I didn't even want to celebrate my birthday anymore. But then ur message came and somehow, in the middle of all that sadness, u made my birthday feel special again 💫

The Reunion Forever 💖

On 2nd April, I realized I just couldn't stay without talking to u anymore. For 4 whole days I searched for ur Snapchat id. After hours of searching I finally found u. I was so happy… but along with that happiness, there was also fear. Would u accept? And u did!!! 💫
